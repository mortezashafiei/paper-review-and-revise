# Field list: geotechnical and offshore engineering

A starting point for venue suggestions (section 13.4 of `SKILL.md`), not a boundary: any other journal or conference can be handled the same way. Before recommending a venue, confirm its current aims and scope, limits and deadlines on its own site, and never quote impact factors or rankings from memory. Match abbreviations and short names against the tables below. Venues with a ready profile are marked with a link to it.

For offshore topics such as piles, anchors, pipelines and foundations for offshore wind, consider the offshore venues alongside the general geotechnical ones.

## Journals: soil mechanics and geotechnical engineering

| Journal | Publisher | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| Géotechnique ([profile](../profiles/geotechnique.yaml)) | ICE Publishing (Emerald) | Fundamental soil mechanics of high rigor, experimental or theoretical, such as stress–strain behavior and anisotropy |
| Géotechnique Letters | ICE Publishing (Emerald) | Short papers intended for rapid publication |
| Journal of Geotechnical and Geoenvironmental Engineering (JGGE) | ASCE | Laboratory testing and applied soil mechanics for researchers and practicing engineers |
| Canadian Geotechnical Journal (CGJ) ([profile](../profiles/canadian-geotechnical-journal.yaml)) | Canadian Science Publishing | Detailed experimental work, including clays, silts and intermediate soils |
| Acta Geotechnica (AG) ([profile](../profiles/acta-geotechnica.yaml)) | Springer Nature | Experimental soil mechanics (triaxial, DSS) tied to constitutive modeling |
| Soils and Foundations | Elsevier, on behalf of the Japanese Geotechnical Society | Soil mechanics and foundation engineering in general |

## Journals: specialized topics

| Journal | Publisher | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| Geotechnical Testing Journal (GTJ) ([profile](../profiles/geotechnical-testing-journal.yaml)) | ASTM International | Test methods, apparatus and procedures for the laboratory and the field |
| Computers and Geotechnics | Elsevier | Numerical and constitutive modeling, including laboratory results used for calibration |
| Soil Dynamics and Earthquake Engineering | Elsevier | Dynamic soil behavior under cyclic loading, including liquefaction |
| Engineering Geology | Elsevier | Geological materials, sites and hazards |
| Journal of Rock Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering (JRMGE) | Elsevier | Rock and soil mechanics |
| International Journal of Geomechanics | ASCE | Geomechanics, analysis and modeling |
| Transportation Geotechnics | Elsevier | Pavements, railways and subgrades |
| Geotextiles and Geomembranes | Elsevier | Geosynthetics |
| Landslides | Springer Nature | Slope instability and landslide processes |
| Geotechnics | MDPI | Open-access journal covering geotechnical engineering |

## Journals: offshore and marine engineering

| Journal | Publisher | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| Ocean Engineering | Elsevier | Offshore engineering: platforms, pipelines and risers, cables and moorings, foundations, scour, offshore renewable energy |
| Applied Ocean Research | Elsevier | Offshore structures and their installation, submarine pipelines, moorings and marine renewable energy |
| Marine Structures | Elsevier | Structures at sea, including seabed foundations and anchoring systems |
| Marine Georesources and Geotechnology | Taylor & Francis | Sediments and rocks of the seafloor and what is built on them: anchors, moorings, bottom installations and pipelines |
| Journal of Offshore Mechanics and Arctic Engineering (JOMAE) | ASME | Offshore mechanics, including offshore geotechnics, soil–pipeline interaction, pipelines and risers |
| Journal of Marine Science and Engineering (JMSE) | MDPI | Open-access journal covering marine science and engineering |

## Conferences: general and specialized

| Conference | Organizer and frequency | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| ICSMGE, International Conference on Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering | ISSMGE, every four years | The flagship international meeting, all topics |
| Geo-Congress ([profile](../profiles/geo-congress.yaml)) | ASCE Geo-Institute, annual | The largest North American geotechnical conference, all topics |
| Canadian Geotechnical Conference, named after its host city (GeoMontréal, for example) | Canadian Geotechnical Society, annual | All topics |
| ECSMGE, European Conference on Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering | ISSMGE, every four years | All topics |
| ARC, Asian Regional Conference on Soil Mechanics and Geotechnical Engineering | ISSMGE, every four years | All topics |
| IS series, International Symposium on Deformation Characteristics of Geomaterials | ISSMGE | Element tests such as triaxial and DSS, small-strain stiffness and anisotropy |
| ICEGE, International Conference on Earthquake Geotechnical Engineering | ISSMGE | Earthquake soil dynamics, cyclic shearing and liquefaction |
| ISC, International Conference on Geotechnical and Geophysical Site Characterization | ISSMGE | In-situ testing and site characterization |
| ICPMG, International Conference on Physical Modelling in Geotechnics | ISSMGE | Centrifuge and physical modeling |
| Geo-Frontiers | ASCE Geo-Institute and GMA, held every few years | Geotechnics together with geosynthetics |
| International Conference on Case Histories in Geotechnical Engineering | Check the current organizer | Field performance and forensic engineering |

## Conferences: offshore and marine

| Conference | Organizer | Typical content |
|---|---|---|
| ISFOG, International Symposium on Frontiers in Offshore Geotechnics | ISSMGE (TC209, Offshore Geotechnics) | Offshore geotechnics: foundations, anchors, pipelines, site characterization, offshore renewables |
| OSIG, International Conference on Offshore Site Investigation and Geotechnics | Society for Underwater Technology (SUT) | Site investigation offshore, together with geophysics and geotechnics |
| OTC, Offshore Technology Conference | OTC, annual in Houston | Offshore industry practice and technology |
| OMAE, International Conference on Ocean, Offshore and Arctic Engineering | ASME, annual | Ocean and offshore engineering, with pipelines, risers and offshore geotechnics among its topics |
| ISOPE, International Ocean and Polar Engineering Conference | ISOPE, annual | Ocean and polar engineering, with geotechnics among its topics |
